List of Rules that apply to the 1000 Achievements SMP Server.

Violating these rules may result in a mute, kick or ban.

[1] Be respectful to others.

[2] Do not cheat.

[3] Do not steal resources from other players.

[4] Do not spam in the chat (message flood).

[5] Do not discuss about politics.

[6] Do not ruin the terrain (don't explode and place random blocks on it).

[7] Do not ask for Admin rank.
